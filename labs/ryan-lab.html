The useful fact can be a combination

Five men on a second roster. “Has a brother in the service” and “that brother is still alive” are both on the form. Neither column alone is Ryan. Both together are.

The last surviving son is the man with a brother who served and who is gone. That is a feature you build. It is not one of the columns.
