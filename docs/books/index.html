Books

Books by Vadim Sokolov and coauthors. Each book is free to read as a PDF, and its source and code are on GitHub.

Computing the Riemann Hypothesis Thorin Measures, Pólya Densities and Hitting Times

Nicholas G. Polson and Vadim Sokolov. 2026. Edition 1.

Computes the Thorin measure whose positivity is equivalent to the Riemann Hypothesis, from Pólya’s integral for Xi in certified ball arithmetic, and proves that finite positivity tests on the heat trace cannot decide it; with hitting times, the reciprocal zeta function and Lee Yang theory.

On the Collatz Conjecture Martingales, Momentum and Continued Fractions

Nick Polson and Vadim Sokolov. 2026. Edition 1.

Why the Collatz (3x+1) conjecture resists proof: the map as a random walk, an optimizer and a Diophantine problem; proofs that fixed horizon monotone Lyapunov certificates and finite residue sieves cannot settle it and the cycle inequality cannot exclude all cycles; a momentum method from log2 3.

Clinical Trials in Practice From Foundations to AI Agents

Alexandra Sokolova and Vadim Sokolov. 2026. Edition 1.

A practical guide to clinical trials as science and as regulated operations: the phases, statistics and design, the daily work of a Phase III study, regulatory submission, and where AI agents belong in clinical development.

On Hilbert’s 6th Problem Probability, Mechanics and Bayesian Computation

Nick Polson and Vadim Sokolov. 2026. Edition 1.

Hilbert’s sixth problem from Kolmogorov’s axioms and the Boltzmann equation to the Navier Stokes equations, and the inverse passage: Bayesian estimation on the solution manifold of a differential equation, where the adjoint evolution of the data is a sufficient statistic for linear equations.
