# Books

Books by Vadim Sokolov and coauthors. Each book is free to read as a PDF, and its source and code are on GitHub.

## [Bayes, Artificial Intelligence, and Deep Learning](https://vsokolov.org/books/bayes_ai_deep_learning/) <small>Foundations of Data Science</small>

[![Bayes, Artificial Intelligence, and Deep Learning: Foundations of Data Science](https://vsokolov.org/books/bayes_ai_deep_learning/cover.png)](https://vsokolov.org/books/bayes_ai_deep_learning/) Nick Polson and Vadim Sokolov. 2026. Edition 1.

A textbook that develops Bayesian reasoning, statistical learning and deep learning as one probabilistic framework, from Bayes' rule and decision theory to neural networks, transformers and AI agents, with code in R and Python.

[![PDF](https://vsokolov.org/books/icon-pdf.svg)](https://github.com/VadimSokolov/bayes_ai_deep_learning/releases/download/v1.0.1/bayes_ai_deep_learning.pdf "PDF") [![Online edition](https://vsokolov.org/books/icon-web.svg)](https://vsokolov.org/bayes_ai_deep_learning/ "Online edition") [![Source on GitHub](https://vsokolov.org/books/icon-github.svg)](https://github.com/VadimSokolov/bayes_ai_deep_learning "Source on GitHub") [![DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23603837](https://zenodo.org/badge/DOI/10.5281/zenodo.23603837.svg)](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23603837)

## [Computing the Riemann Hypothesis](https://vsokolov.org/books/computing_rh/) <small>Thorin Measures, Pólya Densities and Hitting Times</small>

[![Computing the Riemann Hypothesis: Thorin Measures, Pólya Densities and Hitting Times](https://vsokolov.org/books/computing_rh/cover.jpg)](https://vsokolov.org/books/computing_rh/) Nicholas G. Polson and Vadim Sokolov. 2026. Edition 1.

Computes the Thorin measure whose positivity is equivalent to the Riemann Hypothesis, from Pólya's integral for Xi in certified ball arithmetic, and proves that finite positivity tests on the heat trace cannot decide it; with hitting times, friable approximants, the reciprocal zeta function and Lee Yang theory.

[![PDF](https://vsokolov.org/books/icon-pdf.svg)](https://github.com/VadimSokolov/computing_rh/releases/download/v1.4.0/computing_rh.pdf "PDF") [![Source on GitHub](https://vsokolov.org/books/icon-github.svg)](https://github.com/VadimSokolov/computing_rh "Source on GitHub") [![DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23595861](https://zenodo.org/badge/DOI/10.5281/zenodo.23595861.svg)](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23595861) [![Paperback, available at Amazon](https://vsokolov.org/books/available-at-amazon.png)](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0HM6X1WMN "Paperback on Amazon")

## [Clinical Trials in Practice](https://vsokolov.org/books/clinical_trials_in_practice/) <small>From Foundations to AI Agents</small>

[![Clinical Trials in Practice: From Foundations to AI Agents](https://vsokolov.org/books/clinical_trials_in_practice/cover.jpg)](https://vsokolov.org/books/clinical_trials_in_practice/) Alexandra Sokolova and Vadim Sokolov. 2026. Edition 1.

Clinical trials as science and as regulated operations: design and statistics, the daily work of a Phase III study from site selection to the trial master file, regulatory submission, and what AI agents may and may not do in regulated research.

## [On Statistical Methods for Genetics](https://vsokolov.org/books/statistical_methods_genetics/) <small>Fisher, Ewens and the Bayesian Programme</small>

[![On Statistical Methods for Genetics: Fisher, Ewens and the Bayesian Programme](https://vsokolov.org/books/statistical_methods_genetics/cover.jpg)](https://vsokolov.org/books/statistical_methods_genetics/) Nicholas G. Polson. 2026. Edition 1.

Statistical theory of population genetics: an exact form of Fisher's Fundamental Theorem, shrinkage estimates of the rate of adaptation, the Ewens sampling formula and its rigidity, rational conjugacy, and sufficiency for learned coalescent inference.

[![PDF](https://vsokolov.org/books/icon-pdf.svg)](https://github.com/VadimSokolov/statistical_methods_genetics/releases/download/v1.0.1/statistical_methods_genetics.pdf "PDF") [![Source on GitHub](https://vsokolov.org/books/icon-github.svg)](https://github.com/VadimSokolov/statistical_methods_genetics "Source on GitHub") [![DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23509850](https://zenodo.org/badge/DOI/10.5281/zenodo.23509850.svg)](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23509850)

## [On Langlands Conjectures](https://vsokolov.org/books/langlands/) <small>Probabilistic Methods</small>

[![On Langlands Conjectures: Probabilistic Methods](https://vsokolov.org/books/langlands/cover.jpg)](https://vsokolov.org/books/langlands/) Nick Polson and Vadim Sokolov. 2026. Edition 1.

What probability can and cannot contribute to the Langlands programme: Sato Tate laws, random walks on trees and isogeny graphs, Thorin measures and generalized gamma convolutions as reformulations of the Riemann hypothesis, and Bayesian identification of Sato Tate groups, with code and data.

## [On the Collatz Conjecture](https://vsokolov.org/books/collatz/) <small>Martingales, Momentum and Continued Fractions</small>

[![On the Collatz Conjecture: Martingales, Momentum and Continued Fractions](https://vsokolov.org/books/collatz/cover.jpg)](https://vsokolov.org/books/collatz/) Nick Polson and Vadim Sokolov. 2026. Edition 1.

Why the Collatz (3x+1) conjecture resists proof: the map as a random walk, an optimizer and a Diophantine problem; proofs that fixed horizon monotone Lyapunov certificates and finite residue sieves cannot settle it and the cycle inequality cannot exclude all cycles; a momentum method from log2 3.

## [On Hilbert's 6th Problem](https://vsokolov.org/books/hilbert6/) <small>Probability, Mechanics and Bayesian Computation</small>

[![On Hilbert's 6th Problem: Probability, Mechanics and Bayesian Computation](https://vsokolov.org/books/hilbert6/cover.jpg)](https://vsokolov.org/books/hilbert6/) Nick Polson and Vadim Sokolov. 2026. Edition 1.

Hilbert's sixth problem from Kolmogorov's axioms and the Boltzmann equation to the Navier Stokes equations, and the inverse passage: Bayesian estimation on the solution manifold of a differential equation, where the adjoint evolution of the data is a sufficient statistic for linear equations.
